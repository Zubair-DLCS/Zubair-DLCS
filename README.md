### Hi, I'm Zubair 👋

PhD student at the **University of Malakand**, Pakistan. I study how machine learning can be used to secure safety-critical systems.

My background is **ML/DL applications in cybersecurity and information integrity**. I train deep networks to identify, classify and address information/jamming attacks. I look at coordinated campaigns/interferences that drown out real information/voices. 

**My research revolves around one question:** can we detect, measure and remove interference without silencing the information or signal we care about?

#### 🔬 Research interests

#### 📌 Featured projects
**[Beyond Copy-Paste](https://github.com/Zubair-DLCS/beyond-copy-paste)**: detecting coordinated
campaigns that paraphrase with AI. Exact matching recognises 0% of AI rewrites, semantic similarity 91%;
a detector trained on other campaigns holds at 0.97 average precision under combined evasion. Also
measures what privacy protections (hashing, coarse timestamps, differential privacy) cost detection.

**[One Language, Two Borders](https://github.com/Zubair-DLCS/south-asia-discourse)**: online hostility in
Pakistani Roman Urdu and Indian Hindi-English social media. An Indian-trained detector works in
Pakistan (AUC 0.86) but not the reverse (0.71), because the two datasets define hostility
differently, not because the language differs. In Pakistani tweets, naming domestic politicians
raises the odds of abuse 3.7-fold; in Indian posts, hostility rises when Pakistan is mentioned.

**[Two Clocks](https://github.com/Zubair-DLCS/two-clocks)**: forensics of 1.79 M tweets from Russia's Internet
Research Agency. Two natural experiments (US daylight-saving changes and national holidays)
show that the personas posing as Americans don't run on American time. Language models
recover each account's role (macro-F1 0.94), and network analysis exposes siloed units that
relay identical posts within minutes.

**[Engineered Emotion](https://github.com/Zubair-DLCS/engineered-emotion)**: an emotion detector (GoEmotions,
0.59 macro-F1 vs BERT's 0.64) applied to 3,498 IRA Facebook ads with real clicks and spend.
Negative emotion earned 33% higher click odds, and emotion AI systematically under-reads
propaganda, which induces fear without expressing it.

**[Discourse-SINR](https://github.com/Zubair-DLCS/Discourse-SINR)**: treating coordinated influence campaigns as
jammers in the information space. It measures how much coordination drowns out authentic
voices, tests whether detectors survive adaptive adversaries, and quantifies what mitigation
costs real users.
