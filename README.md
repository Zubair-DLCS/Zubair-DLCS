### Hi, I'm Zuabir 👋

PhD student at the **University of Malakand**, Pakistan. My research focus on Trustworthy machine learning
application in safety critical systems. 

My background is **ML/DL application in cybersecurity and information integrity*. I train deep networks to identify, classify and address information/jamming attacks. I look at coordinated campaigns/interferences that drown out real information/voices. 

**The question running through my work:** can we detect, measure and remove interference
without silencing the signal we care about?

---

#### 🔬 Research interests
- Trustworthy and robust ML in adversarial, safety-critical settings
- Detection of jamming, interference and coordinated manipulation
- Computational social science and information integrity
- Spectrogram- and signal-based deep learning (CNNs, Vision Transformers)

#### 📌 Featured project
**[Discourse-SINR](../../discourse-sinr)**: treating coordinated influence campaigns as
jammers in the information space. It measures how much coordination drowns out authentic
voices, tests whether detectors survive adaptive adversaries, and quantifies what mitigation
costs real users.

#### 🛰️ Current work
- **SpectroJam-Net** (HEC Pakistan NRPU project): deep learning for spectrogram-based RF
  jamming detection, plus the SpectroJam-Synth pipeline for synthetic signal datasets
  (QPSK, OFDM, STFT)
- A cross-domain review of interference detection, estimation and removal across RF
  communications and biomedical signals
